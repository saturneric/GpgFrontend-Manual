> "In a world of constant surveillance, everyone deserves a private machine that
> just works."

GpgFrontend is a free, open-source app for OpenPGP encryption, signing, and
key management. OpenPGP is a widely used standard for locking files and
messages, so that only the right people can read them. GpgFrontend puts the
power of the command-line tools into a clean desktop app.

It has a **dual-engine core**. The engine is the part that does the
encryption work. You can use the proven **GnuPG** engine or the modern,
memory-safe **rPGP** engine, which is written in Rust. Everything runs
**locally** on your device, with no accounts, no cloud, and no tracking.

## Features

- **Easy**: encrypt and sign your text, files, and more in just a few clicks.
- **Cross-platform**: runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- **Portable**: run it straight from a USB drive and carry your keys with you.
  See [Portable Mode](/advanced/portable-mode/).
- **Email**: write, sign, encrypt and send email, and read it from your mail
  server. See [Email Overview](/email/overview/).
- **Key databases**: keep keys apart in several separate key databases. See
  [Key Databases](/advanced/key-database/).
- **Dual-engine**: use the mature GnuPG engine or the Rust rPGP engine.
- **Profiles**: keep separate sets of keys and settings, and switch between
  them. See [Profiles](/advanced/profiles/).
- **Future-proof**: try the newest version of the OpenPGP standard (v6,
  RFC 9580) and post-quantum algorithms. These are designed to stay safe even
  against future quantum computers. See
  [Post-Quantum Keys](/guides/post-quantum/).
- **Protected memory**: sensitive data is guarded in secure memory while in
  use. See [Memory Security](/advanced/memory-security/).
- **Private**: everything runs locally, with no telemetry or tracking.
- **Modules**: extra features come as modules. See
  [Module Controller](/advanced/module-controller/).

## Where It Came From

GpgFrontend is inspired by **[gpg4usb][git-gpg4usb]**, a stable, portable,
cross-platform project, and shares its core ideas. GpgFrontend keeps the same
focus on portability. On top of that, it adds advanced features, modern
cryptographic standards, and a clean, easy-to-use interface.

The article _["The Past and Present of GpgFrontend: My Journey with an
Open-Source Encryption Tool"][blog-history]_ tells the story. The project grew
from a simple wish:

> "What if everyone could have a small, reliable, and secure 'crypto machine'?"

That is the heart of the mission: to make OpenPGP encryption truly easy. The
goal is a sturdy, simple, handy "digital machine" for everyone, on any major
operating system.
